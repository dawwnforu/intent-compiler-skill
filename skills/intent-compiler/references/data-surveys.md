# Surveys, forms and analysis

Load only the matching section: survey design, filling a form and analyzing responses are distinct intents. Never invent a user's experiences or classify them from a request for help.

## Fill a form

Require actual questions and supplied facts; determine destination and whether submission is authorized. Inspect branching conditions. Map answers to sources; leave unsupported answers unknown or ask. Distinguish required and optional fields. Draft/fill is not submit. Accept by checking fields, units, conditional questions and unresolved required answers. Stop before unsupported declarations or unauthorized submission.

## Build a questionnaire or quiz

Distinguish entertainment, research and consequential decisions. Invented fun categories are fine when labeled; do not describe them as validated psychological measures. Match questions to the intended construct, avoiding combined questions and misleading proxies.

Define answer types, skip rules and scoring after answer changes. Test branches, back navigation and ties. Before sensitive research collection, determine minimum needed fields, consent/use and storage access; do not add identifiers by default. Use [interactive-products.md](interactive-products.md) for UI execution.

## Analyze data

Inspect schema, observation unit, missing-value codes, duplicates, denominators and multi-select/conditional items before computing. Preserve raw inputs and document deduplication/conflicting records. Same name does not establish duplicate people.

Use the applicable valid base for each proportion; multi-select percentages need not total 100%. Keep skipped/not-applicable separate from zero. Do not compare weekly/monthly counts as equal units. State sample bases on charts and separate observations, interpretation and proposed actions.

Do not infer representativeness, causality, validated scales or intervention effectiveness from a convenience sample/prototype. Missing evidence means limited analysis, not invented records.

## Sources, execution and stop

Start with the instrument, export dictionary and methodology; reuse a spreadsheet/research Skill. Consult applicable method documentation, e.g. [NIST](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/), instead of inventing formulas. Verify changing platform policies or regulated uses as needed.

Recompute key counts from rows, independently check denominators and inspect charts. Supply a reproducible calculation when needed. Stop at the evidence level that answers the request. Extra studies/models remain optional unless necessary.
